## Project Overview

Many small restaurants still take orders by phone and on paper tickets, and pay large fees to third party delivery apps. Our project is a web based online ordering and kitchen inventory system for a small, family owned restaurant.

With this system:

* Customers can view the menu and place pickup or delivery orders from the restaurant's own website.
* Every order, whether it comes from the website, the phone, or the counter, shows up on one kitchen display.
* Ingredient stock is reduced automatically when an order is completed.
* The manager gets an alert when an ingredient is running low.
* The owner can view daily and weekly sales reports.
